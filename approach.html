What to expect

1

Application

Anyone involved can reach out to get the process started. Share what is happening, who else is involved, and the desired outcome from your own perspective.

Depending on the role I will play and the dynamics of the situation, I may require applications from additional parties before I can determine whether I am the right fit for the situation or establish fees and terms.

*Additional information in the FAQ

2

Agreement

If it is a fit, I explain fees and terms. Everyone who participates signs an agreement before we begin.

3

Finding the gap

I speak with each person separately. This may include multiple conversations and questions to answer between conversations.

4

Closing the gap

If appropriate and congruent with the desired outcome, I facilitate the conversation(s) with everyone involved, guiding it toward what it will take to move forward.

If distance is more productive, I help define and facilitate what moving forward looks like for each person.

My commitment

Neutrality. I take no side. My role is understanding every position well enough to translate it.

Honesty. I communicate what I see directly, clearly, and with care, including the parts that are hard to hear.

Confidentiality. What happens in mediation stays in mediation.

Clear boundaries. Legal and financial terms stay with your attorneys and advisors. Clinical care stays with licensed clinicians.

Creativity. I look beyond the obvious and think outside of the box.
