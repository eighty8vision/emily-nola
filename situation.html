Relationships

Something has come between you and the people closest to you. The conversation keeps ending in the same place. Your history makes it hard to stay on track, and you are all too close to what is creating the conflict or tension to see it objectively. The current dynamic is unsustainable and causing more damage than anyone involved wants.

I clarify and define options. Introduce perspective and strategies. Provide objective recommendations and suggestions when it is appropriate, and facilitate communication so it stays productive and in alignment with your stated goals.

  • Partners + spouses
  • Parents + adult children
  • Siblings + extended family
  • In-laws
  • Dynamics related to the loss of a loved one
  • Neighbors
  • Business partners
  • Friends
I work with

All parties. Everyone involved applies and signs.

Not all parties. One person, or a few, when the others are uninterested or unable.

My role

All parties involved: neutral between everyone involved.

Not all parties involved: advisor with objectivity and solution-oriented guidance.

Not my lane

The objective

A path forward, together or apart, reached with less damage. New perspective, improved communication, and strategies you can carry into future conversations.

Workplace

Two or more people, cross-functional teams, or a leader and their team are experiencing challenges that are costing the business.

Interpersonal dynamics and/or structural misalignments are causing distractions, escalations, and risk to the business. Morale is taking a hit, decisions are delayed, and retention of good people is becoming a concern.

I bring an experienced operational leadership and HR lens as an external partner to the business.

Collaborating with leadership, I introduce a fresh and strategic perspective with the goal of diagnosing the source of the issue, recommending solutions, and facilitating the conversations that let everyone move forward and get back to business.

With a people-centered focus, my goal is to find the intersection of what is best for the business, its customers, and its team members. Helping to separate facts from feelings so that emotionally charged situations can gain clarity, and energy toward moving forward can be spent strategically and effectively.

I work with

An organization. Owner, board, leadership, internal HR, fractional HR, COO, CFO, and others in a decision-making role.

An employee. For their own guidance, outside the organization.

My role

An organization: partner and collaborator to the business, objective perspective based on the desired outcome.

An employee: advisor to employee with objectivity and solution-oriented guidance.

Not my lane

Employment law.

The objective

Leadership has a clear understanding of the current situation, risks, and recommendations to move forward. People work together more effectively or move forward separately, organizational structure is modified as needed, and the expense of time, money, energy, and lost opportunity stops.

Negotiations

A divorce, a sale, an agreement, a pending business deal. Negotiations have stalled even though options and possibilities remain on the table.

My focus is on the interpersonal dynamics that fall outside the scope of an attorney's or advisor's role and responsibilities.

I partner with everyone involved to identify what is causing the negotiation to stall and to help shift their footing enough to get the matter moving again.

I do not take a position on the outcome. However, I will learn the situation well enough to understand the necessary context and ensure my involvement is additive rather than another barrier to progress.
