Your situation

  • Efforts and conversations to move forward have started to yield diminishing returns.
  • The damage to relationships risks becoming irreparable.
  • The costs in time, money, and energy have become exorbitant or unnecessary.
  • Decisions get delayed, options narrow, and everyone involved pays for a difficult dynamic that was and is avoidable.

A personal relationship

Something has come between you and the people closest to you. The conversation keeps ending in the same place. Your history makes it hard to stay on track, and you are all too close to what is creating the conflict or tension to see it objectively. The current dynamic is unsustainable and causing more damage than anyone involved wants.

  • Partners + spouses
  • Parents + adult children
  • Siblings + extended family
  • In-laws
  • Navigating the passing of a loved one
  • Neighbors
  • Business partners
  • Friends

I work with everyone involved as a neutral third party, without the clinical frame of therapy.

I translate between the parties, so each side understands what the other is actually asking for. Clarify and define options. Provide objective recommendations and suggestions when it is appropriate, and facilitate communication so it stays productive and in alignment with your stated goals.

This tends to fit when

  • Everyone involved is willing to participate and be open
  • There is an objective or a goal brought to the conversation to work towards
  • Perspectives, needs, or desired outcomes vary

An organization

Two or more people, cross-functional teams, or a leader and their team are experiencing challenges that are costing the business. Interpersonal dynamics are distracting others and the work. Morale is taking a hit, decisions get delayed, and good people are starting to look elsewhere.

I work as a neutral third party with an experienced operational and HR lens. Partnered with decision-making business leaders and the individuals involved, I bring a fresh perspective with the goal of diagnosing the source of the issue, identifying solutions, and supporting the impacted individuals in a way that lets everyone move forward and get back to business.

What makes this different

  • I look at the work and the process alongside the people, since friction often starts in how work is structured
  • Each person gets a private conversation first, so the full picture surfaces before anyone is in a room together
  • Leadership receives a clear read on what is happening and what it will take to resolve

A stalled negotiation

A divorce, a contract, a pending business deal. Counsel is engaged and the matter is approaching or at a deadlock, even though options and possibilities remain on the table.

I work alongside the attorneys or advisors as a neutral third party, leaving every legal and financial term with them. I bring a fresh perspective to all impacted parties with the goal of getting to the source of what is stalling them, to help them shift their footing enough to get the matter moving again.

For attorneys and advisors

  • You keep full ownership of the legal and financial substance
  • I focus on the human dynamics holding the matter in place
  • A matter that moves again saves your client time, cost, and escalation

A stuck client

You are an attorney or advisor with a client who is holding their own matter in place. They circle, stall, or resist a resolution that is within reach, and you have run out of ways to help them see it.

I work with your client directly on what is keeping them stuck, with the goal of shifting perspectives so they are able to move forward in the way that is most holistically advantageous to them.

How this works alongside you

  • You make the introduction and remain your client's advocate
  • My work stays on their thinking and their footing, never the terms
  • Your client arrives at their decisions with a clearer head
